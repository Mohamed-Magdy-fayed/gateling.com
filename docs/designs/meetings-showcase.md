# Design: Showcasing Gateling Meetings on gateling.com

## Problem Statement

Gateling Meetings (meetings.gateling.com) is a self-hosted, Arabic-first video meeting product that Gateling built and runs. gateling.com sells custom software to business owners, and nowhere on the site does it show that Gateling built Meetings. The goal is to use Meetings as **proof of what Gateling can build**, so that it drives consultations. Selling Meetings seats is not the goal.

## Demand Evidence

- Since the integration shipped, every booked consultation and every scheduled sales demo runs in a Gateling Meetings room (`docs/meetings-integration.md`).
- **A real prospect noticed the room during a call and asked about it.** That's the strongest signal we have: the proof landed while they were inside the product, not while reading about it.
- Gap: the prospect's exact words haven't been captured yet (see Assignment).

## Target User

A business owner who wants **their own platform**, for example an academy that wants its own branded live classroom, a clinic that wants private consultation rooms, or a company that wants its own sales demo room. They speak in outcomes, not tech (`docs/public-experience.md`, Copy Tone).

## Premises (agreed)

1. The showcase sells custom builds, not Meetings seats. The one primary call to action is "Book a consultation", and that booking lands the visitor in a Meetings room. meetings.gateling.com appears only as a secondary "see it live" link. No pricing on gateling.com.
2. Lead with "you're already inside it": every Gateling call runs on software Gateling built. The prospect's reaction backs this up, quoted with permission or anonymized.
3. Speak the owner's world: describe 2-3 scenarios (classroom, consultation room, demo room). The tech (LiveKit, webhooks, Paymob) goes in one short "under the hood" strip.
4. Honest framing: the "client" is Gateling. The story is "we built and run this ourselves, and we can build yours." It never passes as a client engagement.
5. Reuse what exists: the `/work` case study plus content blocks. No schema change.

## Approaches Considered

- **A: Flagship case study only.** Content-only, so it's cheapest. Not chosen on its own, because the proof is only told in text and never experienced. It becomes part 1 of B.
- **C: `/solutions/video-platforms` page + public demo room.** This is the SEO play. Deferred: it takes the most effort, a public demo room needs abuse and cost limits plus a security review, and it drifts toward selling Meetings.

## Recommended Approach: B (Case study + "you're inside it" loop)

**Why:** the evidence is a prospect reacting while *inside* a call. B turns that accident into a moment every booked prospect gets, with small effort and no migration.

### Part 1: Case study `/work/gateling-meetings` (content, admin block editor)

Mark it `featured` so it appears in the homepage `work-preview-section.tsx`. Block order:

1. **Hero:** "We built our own video meeting platform. You'll meet us in it." Key metric callout uses a real number (e.g. calls hosted since launch; see Open Questions).
2. **Challenge (`paragraph`):** why a business ends up stuck with Zoom or Meet: someone else's branding, per-host fees, data on someone else's servers, no Arabic.
3. **Solution (`device_player` / `video`):** a 30-60s screen recording of a guest joining from the browser with no account, the waiting room, the host admitting them, screen share with annotation.
4. **Scenarios (`list` or `comparison`):** classroom, consultation room, sales demo room. One line each, in the owner's language.
5. **Proof (`quote`):** the prospect's reaction.
6. **Results (`stats`):** real numbers only, e.g. bookings hosted, EN/AR, $0/month infra on free tiers, guest join with no account.
7. **Under the hood (`callout`):** one short strip: real-time video (LiveKit), integrations and webhooks, local payments (Paymob, EGP).
8. **CTA (`cta`):** "Want a platform like this for your business? Book a call. It'll be in a Meetings room." Secondary link: meetings.gateling.com.

Both EN and AR versions are required.

### Part 2: The loop (code, gateling.com)

Add a single line, "Your call runs on Gateling Meetings, software we built. See how →", linking to `/work/gateling-meetings`, at:

- **Booking confirmation email:** `src/integrations/inngest/functions/on-booking-confirmed.ts` (customer email, only when a `meetingGuestUrl` exists).
- **My Account join-call card:** `src/app/(landing-pages)/my-account/_components/my-bookings.tsx`.
- **(Optional) Sales demo:** the guest link copied from `demo-meeting-card.tsx` can carry the same line in the suggested WhatsApp text.

Every string goes through i18n with keys added to both `en` and `ar`, and the layout is RTL-safe. Run `/ui-scan --plan` before the UI work and `/ui-scan` after.

### Part 3 (optional, separate repo): Meetings waiting room

While a guest waits in a room hosted by the `gateling-website` integration, show "Built by Gateling: see the story" in the waiting state (`gateling-meetings/src/features/meetings/components/meeting-client.tsx` or wherever the waiting state renders). It must be **scoped to that integration only**, never shown to other Meetings customers. This is a change to the Meetings repo, so it gets its own branch.

## Open Questions

1. Can we quote the prospect by name, or only anonymized ("Operations manager, training company")?
2. What real usage number goes in the hero and stats (bookings or demos hosted since the integration went live)? Pull it from `bookings` where `meetingCode` is not null. That's a read-only query against production Neon, so confirm first.
3. Part 3: is the waiting-room line worth a cross-repo change in v1, or should it ship after Parts 1-2 prove the loop?
4. Should the case-study template allow "Gateling (in-house product)" as the client/industry, or does that need a small form tweak?

## Success Criteria

- The case study is published in EN and AR, featured on the homepage, and indexed in the sitemap.
- The confirmation email and My Account both link to it whenever a room exists.
- Measured over the next 30 days: visits to `/work/gateling-meetings` coming from the email or My Account link, and how many consultation prospects mention Meetings on the call.

## Dependencies

- Screen recording of the guest flow (human).
- The prospect's quote and consent (human).
- Branch flow: `feat/meetings-showcase` → `preview` → `main`.

## The Assignment

Before writing a single block: **message the prospect who noticed the room**. Ask what they thought when they realized Gateling built it, and whether you can quote them. Their exact words become the hero line and the `quote` block.

## What I noticed about how you think

- When asked what Meetings should do on the site, you chose "Capability proof" over selling it, even though it already has working Paymob checkout. You kept the agency's real business ahead of a side revenue stream.
- Your evidence was behavior, not opinion: "Prospect noticed it." That one reaction decided the whole design.
- You named the buyer as a category ("owner wanting own platform"), not a person. The Assignment exists to close that gap.
