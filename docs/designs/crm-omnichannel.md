## Problem Statement

Mohamed is the whole company: sales, CEO, developer. He sells Gateling software to
Egyptian ateliers by **cold outreach, mostly WhatsApp from his WhatsApp Business app
number**, plus calls and Instagram/Facebook/TikTok profiles he finds on Maps and IG.

The current sales module (`/sales/today`, `/sales/leads`, `src/features/system/sales/**`)
is a call-list engine that never sees a message. Every send and every reply happens on
his phone and must be re-typed as an outcome. He said all four of these are true:

- **Conversations live elsewhere.** The app doesn't see replies; logging is double work and gets skipped.
- **The Today list is wrong.** He doesn't trust what it tells him to do next.
- **He can't see the lead.** No at-a-glance identity: socials, location, logo, history.
- **No reminders he trusts.**

Plus, in his words: "the look and feel of the app is all AI Slop i need something that
looks nothing like allover the place AI stuff".

Diagnosis: the first problem causes the other three. The bucket rules
(`lib/today-rules.ts`) and the 2-unanswered WhatsApp safety rule are sound but run on
data that is missing whenever he skips logging. So Today is wrong and reminders fire at
the wrong time.

## Demand Evidence

Single user, the founder himself, who works leads daily during the sell-first sprint.
Evidence is behavioural: he skips manual logging, which is why `followUpCount` and
`lastContactedAt` drift from reality. He has built and used the same Meta pattern
before (megz-courses: page-token exchange, one webhook for `page`,
`whatsapp_business_account` and `instagram`, inbound auto-creates a lead), so the
capability is proven by him, not hypothetical.

## Status Quo

Message from the phone (WhatsApp Business app, sometimes IG/FB apps), then later open
`/sales/today` and tap an outcome, or forget to. Lead research (socials, followers) is
written by the sales MCP agent into `leads.socialProfiles`; nothing about messages is.

## Target User & Narrowest Wedge

User: Mohamed, working 10–30 cold atelier leads a day, alone, on phone + laptop.
Wedge: **passive WhatsApp capture.** Every WhatsApp message he sends from his phone and
every reply lands on the right lead and logs itself as an activity. That one change
makes Today, follow-up counts, reminders and the ban-safety rule accurate without
touching their logic.

## Premises (agreed)

1. One **CRM** menu tab replaces `/sales/today` + `/sales/leads`. Its home is a rebuilt
   **Focus** queue, the existing bucket engine fed by real message data. *Revised in
   session after the second opinion: keep the queue engine, not an inbox-first home.*
   The append-only activity log, the 2-unanswered safety rule and the lead record stay.
2. The conversation thread is the heart of a lead. Every message on any channel lands
   in one timeline per lead. Activities are **derived from messages** (a reply resets
   follow-ups) instead of logged by hand.
3. WhatsApp through coexistence on his Business number is the only channel v1 must get
   working. Messenger + Instagram for his own Page come second, after a spike. TikTok
   and Snapchat get an adapter slot now (deep-link only) and a real connection later.
4. Cold outreach on IG/FB/TikTok stays a one-click deep link (`ig.me`, `m.me`, profile
   URL), logged as an attempt.
5. The visual redesign is in scope (slice 2), not later polish. The lead page is built
   around identity: logo, brand-coloured socials with follower counts, and a map.

## Approaches Considered

### Approach A: WhatsApp capture only (minimal viable)
Coexistence webhook → messages → derived activities → existing Today. No new shell.
M effort (human ~1 week / CC ~1 day). Fixes the data, but leaves the disliked screens
and has no sending. It became slice 1 of B.

### Approach B: Channel-adapter CRM in 4 shippable slices (CHOSEN)
New `/crm` area; channel-agnostic messaging core; one adapter per channel. L effort
(human ~4–5 weeks / CC ~5–7 days across slices). Details below.

### Approach C: Self-hosted Chatwoot as the message engine
Rejected. It's another service to host (Rails + Redis + Postgres), means two UIs and a
look that isn't his, and its coexistence-echo support is unverified. It breaks
"operations from a single source".

Also rejected: unofficial WhatsApp-Web gateways (terms of service + ban risk).

## Recommended Approach

### Module layout (developer clarity first)

```
src/features/system/messaging/        ← channel-agnostic core, no UI
  channels/types.ts                   ← ChannelAdapter interface (below)
  channels/registry.ts                ← channel id → adapter; the ONE place to add Snapchat
  channels/whatsapp/                  ← verify, normalize (messages, smb_message_echoes,
                                         history), send (text in window, template outside)
  channels/messenger/                 ← slice 4
  channels/instagram/                 ← slice 4
  channels/tiktok/, channels/snapchat/← deep-link-only adapters (send = unsupported)
  lib/identity.ts                     ← pure: phone/handle normalisation + match rules
  lib/derive-activity.ts              ← pure: message(s) → lead_activities rows (burst rule)
  lib/window.ts                       ← pure: is the reply window open, until when
  server/ingest.ts                    ← Inngest fn: webhook_event → messages → activities
  server/send.ts                      ← send path; enforces DNC + safety rule server-side
  server/channel-accounts.ts          ← token exchange, encrypted storage, health
src/app/api/webhooks/meta/route.ts    ← GET verify challenge; POST: check signature
                                         (invalid → 401, body NOT persisted), store raw
                                         event, send Inngest event, 200 fast. Rate-limited
                                         with the existing Redis limiter (src/integrations/redis.ts)
src/features/system/crm/              ← UI: Focus, Leads, Lead page, Follow-ups,
                                         Unmatched, Channels (slice 2+)
src/features/system/sales/            ← stays the domain engine: today-rules, derived,
                                         pipeline-status, logActivity, MCP
```

```ts
interface ChannelAdapter {
  id: Channel;                                   // whatsapp | messenger | instagram | tiktok | snapchat
  verifySignature(req: RawRequest): boolean;     // X-Hub-Signature-256 for Meta
  normalize(payload: unknown): NormalizedEvent[];// messages, echoes, statuses, history
  send?(account, identity, content): Promise<SendResult>; // absent = deep-link only
  replyWindowHours: number | null;               // 24 (WA/FB/IG), 48 (TikTok), null
  deepLink(identity | profile): string;          // wa.me / m.me / ig.me / profile URL
}
```

Adding a channel = one adapter folder + one registry line + one enum value. Nothing
else in the CRM changes.

### Data model (via `/migration`, generated, never hand-written)

| Table | Purpose | Key columns |
|---|---|---|
| `webhook_events` | Raw inbox, used for idempotency and replay. Only signature-valid requests are ever written. | channel, receivedAt, payload jsonb, processedAt, error. Raw payloads purged after 30 days (PII). |
| `channel_accounts` | A connected number/page/IG account | channel, externalId (phone_number_id / page id / IG id), displayName, accessTokenEncrypted, tokenExpiresAt, status (connected/expired/error), lastEventAt |
| `lead_identities` | Who this person is on each channel, lead or not | leadId nullable, channel, externalUserId (wa_id / PSID / IGSID), handle, displayName, matchedBy (auto_phone / manual / inbound_created), ignored boolean, needsReview boolean; unique (channel, externalUserId) |
| `messages` | Every message, any channel | leadId nullable (null = unmatched), identityId, channelAccountId, channel, direction (in/out), origin (api / app_echo / history_sync / chat_export), externalMessageId unique, body, media jsonb, deliveryStatus, occurredAt, activityId |

- No `conversations` table. A lead's thread is its messages ordered by `occurredAt`;
  the unmatched tray is messages with `leadId IS NULL`, grouped by identity. The reply
  window is derived from the last inbound message per lead + channel.
- `lead_activities` stays append-only. WhatsApp messages map to the existing
  `whatsapp_sent` / `whatsapp_reply`, so the safety rule and `derived.ts` keep their
  meaning unchanged. Other channels get new types `message_sent` / `message_received`
  and the channel enum gains `messenger`, `instagram`, `tiktok`, `snapchat`.
  `derived.ts` classifies them the same way: received = conversation, sent = attempt.
- `leads` gains `logoUrl` (Firebase, through the existing storage helper) and
  `placeId` / `lat` / `lng` for the map, if `lib/places-leads.ts` doesn't already
  carry them.

### Derivation rules (pure, unit-tested, the riskiest logic)

- **Burst rule:** consecutive outbound messages to one lead within 30 minutes with no
  inbound in between count as ONE attempt. Without this, a 3-message intro counts as
  3 chases and Auto-park fires wrongly. The threshold lives in config, like
  `TodayRulesConfig`.
- Inbound message = conversation: resets `followUpCount`, discharges promises, and if
  status is `awaiting_reply` → `contacted` (a `status_change` activity, never an edit).
- Idempotency is keyed on `externalMessageId`: the same webhook delivered twice is a
  no-op. Echo and API send of the same message dedupe on wamid.
- Delivery/read status updates `messages.deliveryStatus` only, never an activity.

**Incremental algorithm (handles retries and out-of-order delivery):**
1. The ingest Inngest function runs with concurrency 1 per `leadId`, so one lead's
   messages are never derived in parallel. Unmatched messages derive nothing.
2. Insert the message (no-op on a duplicate `externalMessageId`).
3. `deriveGroups(messages)` is pure. It takes ALL of the lead's messages ordered by
   `occurredAt` (never receipt order) and returns groups: one per inbound message, and
   one per outbound burst, bounded by inbound messages and the 30-minute gap. Each
   group's `sourceKey` = the `externalMessageId` of its earliest message.
4. `lead_activities` gains a nullable unique `sourceKey` column. For each group with no
   activity carrying that `sourceKey`, append one activity with `occurredAt` = the
   group's first message. Set `messages.activityId` for every member. A late outbound
   that lands inside an existing burst just joins it (same `sourceKey`) and creates no
   activity.
5. A late inbound that splits an already-logged burst leaves the old activity as is
   (append-only). The part of the burst after that inbound becomes a new group with its
   own `sourceKey`, so it gets its own activity.
6. Then run `recomputeLeadDerived()`. It already orders activities by `occurredAt`, so
   an earlier-occurring inbound that arrives late still resets `followUpCount`
   correctly. The `awaiting_reply → contacted` `status_change` is only appended when
   the newest group is inbound, which avoids flip-flopping on late history.
7. **Groups are frozen once logged.** `deriveGroups` receives the lead's
   already-logged `sourceKey`s and never re-merges them. A late outbound that would
   bridge two logged bursts (bursts at 10:00 and 10:40, an echo at 10:20) joins the
   earlier group and creates no activity. Both existing attempts stand. This is an
   accepted, rare over-count of at most one attempt, and only when an echo is very
   late. The fix is always more data, never an edit to the log.
8. Unit tests over fixtures:
   - echo arriving after the reply
   - a duplicate retry
   - history interleaved with live events
   - a late inbound splitting a burst
   - a late outbound bridging two logged bursts
   - a burst of 3 sends

**Reconciliation with manually logged history (no double counting):**
- `CAPTURE_STARTED_AT` is the **slice-1 go-live moment**: the deploy that removes the
  manual WhatsApp outcomes. It's stored on `channel_accounts` (that table arrives in
  slice 1, not when the number was onboarded in the spike) and is visible in
  `/crm/channels`. Messages replayed from the spike period are therefore history.
- Messages that occurred **before** `CAPTURE_STARTED_AT` (history sync, plus live
  echoes and replies captured between the spike and go-live) are stored and
  shown in the thread but derive **no** activities. The manual log stays the truth for
  that period, so nothing is counted twice. No recompute and no status changes happen
  for them.
- **After** go-live, the manual WhatsApp outcomes ("Sent", "Replied") are removed from
  the outcome sets in `lib/outcomes.ts`. The service also refuses manual
  `whatsapp_sent` / `whatsapp_reply` for a lead whose number has a connected,
  live-capturing account (new `SalesError` code `SALES_WHATSAPP_CAPTURED`). The same
  applies to the MCP `log_outcome`. Call outcomes are untouched.

**Non-lead traffic on the business number:**
- History sync writes only messages whose identity matches a lead into `messages`.
  Everything else is never stored in `messages`. The raw payloads (which include
  non-lead conversations) remain only in `webhook_events`, readable by DB query, until
  the processed-row purge deletes them 30 days after processing. Non-lead content
  exists in exactly two places: (1) raw `webhook_events` rows, until 30 days after
  processing (60 days at most); (2) bodies of live messages from unknown numbers in
  `messages` (`leadId = null`, the Unmatched tray), for 14 days. Both are accepted
  for debugging and triage, and are stated in the security review.
- Live messages from an unknown number go to the Unmatched tray, which has three
  actions: "Create lead", "Attach to lead", "Ignore this number". Ignore sets
  `lead_identities.ignored = true`, and from then on that number's messages are
  dropped at ingest.
- Unmatched message bodies are purged after 14 days by the same daily Inngest cron
  that purges `webhook_events`.

### Safety rule with phone-app sends

The server cannot block a message typed in the phone app. In slice 1 the protection is
detection, early enough to act on:
- Captured sends feed `followUpCount`, so a lead with 2 unanswered attempts moves into
  the Auto-park bucket, where the UI offers Park and never another message.
- The lead page shows "2 of 2 unanswered — do not message" in the status band.
- Measurement query (`docs/queries/crm-safety.sql`): leads with 3 or more consecutive
  unanswered outbound groups since `CAPTURE_STARTED_AT`. The target is 0.
- Server-side refusal applies to in-app sends from slice 3 on.

### Chat-export fallback contract

Used only if coexistence fails in the spike. The import runs **from the lead page**, so
the lead is known and no identity matching is needed:
- WhatsApp `.txt` timestamps are parsed in Africa/Cairo for both Arabic and English
  export formats; an unknown format is refused.
- `externalMessageId = "export:" + sha256(leadId | occurredAt-minute | direction | body)`,
  so re-importing an overlapping export skips messages it already has.
- Derived activities are created only for messages after the lead's latest manual
  WhatsApp activity, so nothing is counted twice.

### Identity matching

- WhatsApp: `wa_id` (E.164 digits) is matched against a **comparison-only**
  normalisation of `leads.phone` / `phoneSecondary` across `kind = 'prospect'` leads.
  Stored phones are never rewritten (house rule). Egyptian 01x… ↔ 201x… is covered by
  unit tests.
- Matching happens once per identity, not once per message:
  - **Exactly one** lead matches → write a `lead_identities` row
    (`matchedBy = auto_phone`). Later messages use that row and never re-match.
  - **Zero or several** leads match → the identity stays `leadId = null` and goes to
    the Unmatched tray. When several match, the tray lists the candidate leads, and a
    human picks one.
  - "Attach to lead" writes `matchedBy = manual` and backfills that identity's
    messages, running the incremental algorithm for the lead.
- When a lead's phone changes, any `auto_phone` identity that no longer matches gets
  `needsReview = true` and shows in the tray. It is never silently moved. There is no
  lead-merge feature today, so merges are out of scope.
- Messenger/IG inbound from a stranger creates a `prospect` lead (source
  `messenger`/`instagram`) unless the handle matches `socialProfiles`.

### Slices (each ships alone, `feat/crm-<slice>` → `preview` → `main`)

**Slice 0 — Spike (gate, ~1 day, minimal durable capture code).** This decides
everything. History sync is delivered once, at onboarding, so the spike must persist
what it receives. A throwaway logger would lose the history for good.
1. Ship the smallest durable piece of slice 1 first, on `feat/crm-capture-spike` →
   `preview` → `main`:
   - the `webhook_events` table (generated migration);
   - `/api/webhooks/meta`: GET verify challenge; POST checks the signature, returns
     401 on an invalid one and persists nothing; valid → stores raw JSON and returns
     200. No Inngest processing yet.
   - rate limiting;
   - the daily purge cron. It deletes only **processed** rows
     (`processedAt IS NOT NULL` and older than 30 days), so nothing is lost before
     slice 1 replays it. Slice 1 must replay within **21 days** of onboarding. If it
     slips past that, the spike rows stay until they're replayed; they are never
     purged unprocessed. Terminal ingest failures (malformed payload, adapter bug,
     exhausted Inngest retries) set `error` and show as a count in `/crm/channels`, so
     they can be replayed after a fix. Any row older than 60 days is purged whether
     processed or not, which caps raw PII retention.
   Raw payloads can be read only by a DB query; no UI or API exposes them. The
   **security review (agent `security`) runs before merge to `main`**, because this
   endpoint receives third-party PII.
2. Create one Meta app (dev mode) with the WhatsApp, Messenger and Instagram products.
3. Onboard the Business number with coexistence. If self-serve Embedded Signup needs
   Tech Provider status he doesn't have, try a BSP that supports coexistence (e.g.
   360dialog) forwarding webhooks to the same route.
4. The webhook lives on the **production domain path**. Vercel preview deployments
   sit behind Deployment Protection, which Meta can't pass. Slice 1 then **replays**
   every stored `webhook_events` row through the ingest pipeline, so history and every
   message between spike and slice 1 survive.
4. Pass criteria:
   - (a) a reply from a real non-role contact arrives as a `messages` webhook;
   - (b) a message sent from the phone app arrives as `smb_message_echoes`;
   - (c) history sync delivers at least one past message;
   - (d) a Messenger message from a non-role FB account to his Page arrives while the
     app is in dev mode.
- (a)+(b) pass → slice 1 as designed.
- (a)/(b) fail in Egypt → fallback: the slice-1 pipeline ingests WhatsApp `.txt` chat
  exports (origin `chat_export`) through the same normalize → derive path. It's
  manual, but logs a whole chat in one go.
- (d) fails → slice 4 waits for business verification + App Review.

## Success Criteria

- Within 7 days of slice 1 on production:
  - ≥ 90% of WhatsApp messages to or from leads appear on the right lead with no manual
    logging (spot-check 20 threads on the phone against the CRM);
  - manual `whatsapp_sent`/`whatsapp_reply` logs drop to ~0;
  - every lead with 2 unanswered captured attempts shows in Auto-park within one
    ingest cycle, and the `crm-safety.sql` query (3+ consecutive unanswered outbound
    groups since go-live) returns 0 rows. That's detection, not prevention: phone-app
    sends can't be blocked by the server.
- After slice 2: he works the day from `/crm` Focus and no longer opens the old Today
  page (it redirects). "Replied" items are answered the same business day.
- After slice 3: no promise or follow-up passes its due time without a reminder having
  fired.
- Adding a deep-link-only channel touches only `messaging/channels/<id>/`,
  `registry.ts`, the enum migration and its i18n labels.
