| Reason | Pages | Read |
|---|---|---|

**There is no "Soft 404" row.** The unfixed soft-404 defect on `/blog/[slug]` and
`/work/[slug]` (see `seo-blueprint.md`) has **not** yet been classified by Google as a soft
404 and is costing nothing measurable today. That materially lowers its priority relative
to the 19 discovered-not-indexed pages. Re-check this row on every future measurement — if
it starts climbing, the root-layout refactor moves up the list.

`Discovered – currently not indexed` shows validation **Started**, so a validation run is
already in flight.

| `tms.gateling.com/student/courses/ram` | 0 | 1 | 4 |
| `gateling.com/sign-in` | 0 | 1 | 8 |

Three things worth acting on:

1. **`www.gateling.com/` appears separately at position 49.** Confirm www redirects to
   non-www with a 301 and that canonicals point at the non-www origin. Two origins competing
   is a self-inflicted split.
2. **Client subdomains are indexed and outranking the marketing site** — `tms.`, `emanz.`,
   `atelier.` collect 23 impressions between them and produced the only non-homepage click.
   Decide deliberately whether client apps should be indexed at all; `tms.gateling.com/
   authentication?variant=login` and `gateling.com/sign-in` ranking are pure waste.
3. **`/solutions/delivery` already registers at position 10** with no article support and no
   internal links beyond the footer. The pillar pattern works — this is the strongest
   evidence for prioritising Phase 1 and Phase 6.

## What this baseline changes about the plan

- **Phase 1 (`/services/[slug]` + internal linking) is confirmed as the top priority.**
  19 pages sitting at "Discovered – currently not indexed" is the signature of thin internal
  linking, which is exactly what Phase 1 addresses.
- **The soft-404 refactor drops in priority** — no soft 404s are being reported.
- **Two new items** not in the original plan, both cheap: verify the www → non-www redirect,
  and decide whether client subdomains should be indexed.
- Success criteria for the next measurement: any non-branded impression at all;
  "Discovered – currently not indexed" trending down; indexed count above 10.
